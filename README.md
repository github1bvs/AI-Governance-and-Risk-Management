# AI Governance and Risk Management

## Project Overview

The AI Governance and Risk Management project aims to identify, evaluate, and address risks from artificial intelligence systems, including generative AI and agentic AI. This framework helps ensure that AI is developed and used responsibly, transparently, and in line with regulation, contributing to trustworthy adoption across the organization.

## Objectives

- Identify AI risks affecting the organization.
- Assess the impact and likelihood of AI system risks.
- Develop and implement governance controls and mitigation strategies.
- Monitor and review AI systems and controls regularly.

## Deliverables

- **AI Risk Assessment Report**: Document detailing identified AI risks, their impact, and likelihood.
- **AI System Inventory**: A centralized register of AI systems, models, owners, and risk ratings.
- **AI Governance Policy**: Policies for acceptable use, transparency, human oversight, and incident handling.
- **AI Risk Management Plan**: Comprehensive plan outlining the process for governing and monitoring AI risks.

## Project Phases

### 1. AI Discovery and Inventory

- **Objective**: Identify all AI systems and use cases in the organization.
- **Activities**:
  - Inventory AI models, tools, and vendors.
  - Classify AI systems by risk level and regulatory scope.
  - Document data sources, owners, and intended use.

### 2. Risk Assessment

- **Objective**: Evaluate the impact and likelihood of AI risks.
- **Activities**:
  - Perform risk assessments aligned to NIST AI RMF.
  - Assess bias, privacy, security, and accuracy risks.
  - Prioritize AI risks based on severity and regulatory exposure.

### 3. Mitigation Planning

- **Objective**: Develop controls and strategies to mitigate AI risks.
- **Activities**:
  - Define guardrails, human-in-the-loop controls, and testing requirements.
  - Develop incident response plans for AI failures.
  - Assign responsibilities for AI oversight.

### 4. Implementation

- **Objective**: Implement AI governance controls.
- **Activities**:
  - Execute governance policies and control measures.
  - Monitor the performance and outputs of AI systems.
  - Adjust controls as needed based on real-world behavior.

### 5. Monitoring and Review

- **Objective**: Continuously monitor and review AI risks and controls.
- **Activities**:
  - Regularly update the AI system inventory and risk register.
  - Conduct periodic reviews and audits of AI governance.
  - Communicate AI risk status and updates to stakeholders.
